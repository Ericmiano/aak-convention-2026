# Production Dependencies Identified by the 2026 Audit

## Members Platform and Registration Continuity

The public Convention site intentionally does not imitate ticket availability, payment, attendee-data collection, or confirmation because those actions currently belong to the AAK member platform. Before launch, the members-platform event page needs a matching Convention visual treatment and removal of the Nairobi Biennale promotional banner. The practical options are to reskin the event and booking templates on `members.aak.or.ke`, or to expose approved registration and payment APIs so the Convention site can host a real ticket-to-confirmation flow.

## Canonical Event Facts

AAK should confirm one canonical venue property name and date range before either is committed in booking confirmations, attendee emails, or new public copy. The public site continues to use the event-level Diani, Kenya label and the 16–19 September date range pending that confirmation.

## Required Content Inputs

The speakers page will remain an explicit Secretariat-confirmed placeholder until approved names, roles, organisations, biographies, and portrait permissions are supplied.

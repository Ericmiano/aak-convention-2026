# AAK Annual Convention 2026 — Design Directions

## Three possible directions

### 1. Coastal Civic Modernism
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, architectural editorial experience that joins Kenyan civic confidence with Diani's coastal light. It moves from fractured typographic fields toward calmer, connected spatial compositions as visitors progress through the site.

**Probability:** 0.047

### 2. Archive of Resilience
**Very Brief Intro:** A research-publication aesthetic built from annotated imagery, document margins, and institutional archival cues. The experience would foreground the Convention's intellectual agenda through a deliberately measured, reference-led visual language.

**Probability:** 0.082

### 3. Tectonic Field
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast system of structural lines, dimensional type, and dark material surfaces that turns the notion of fragility-to-resilience into a more dramatic visual progression. It is energetic and spatial, with visual emphasis on construction and transformation.

# Selected Direction: Coastal Civic Modernism

## Design Movement

The website will use **contemporary African editorial design with civic-modernist architectural cues**. It avoids both travel-brochure imagery and generic conference UI by treating the Convention as a cultural and professional gathering with a serious intellectual programme, framed by the light, material texture, and landscape of the Kenyan coast.

## Core Principles

1. **Structure in transition:** The visual sequence moves from offset and fragmented rules in the hero toward clearer alignment and connection deeper in the page, expressing the theme without relying on decorative illustration.
2. **Editorial hierarchy before interface chrome:** Large display type, metadata, captions, image crops, and measured rules do the primary work; conventional cards are used only where an interaction requires them.
3. **Place without cliché:** Coastal tones and documentary architecture photography evoke Diani, Kenya, and the built environment without resort imagery or generic "African conference" visual tropes.
4. **Institutional clarity:** Navigation, forms, programme controls, and calls to action remain highly legible, direct, keyboard-accessible, and deliberate.

## Color Philosophy

AAK's existing warm red/burgundy remains the site’s decisive action and identity color, used sparingly to signal registration, active programme states, and structural tension. Warm mineral white makes the site feel paper-like rather than sterile, while ink charcoal anchors the editorial typography. Sand, oxidized terracotta, and a restrained deep coastal teal may appear as supporting contextual tones. The palette should communicate authority, place, and material resilience—not luxury hospitality and not tech futurism.

## Layout Paradigm

The website follows an **offset editorial spine** instead of a centered content grid. Sections align to alternating vertical rails, with a thin numbered index at the outer edge on large screens. Images deliberately overshoot their text columns, and key metadata is placed in fixed-width architectural bands. On mobile, the spine becomes a clear stacked sequence with a persistent registration action, rather than a compressed desktop grid.

## Signature Elements

1. **Tectonic rules:** Thin burgundy and charcoal lines begin separated in the hero and resolve into aligned section dividers and programme connectors.
2. **Cropped documentary frames:** Imagery is carried in slightly offset rectangular frames with precise captions, avoiding soft rounded image treatments.
3. **Indexed editorial labels:** Page sections use understated numerical labels such as `01 / THE CONVENTION`, building a navigable publication-like rhythm.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should expose information as though unfolding a well-designed event programme: programme rows extend with supporting context, navigation becomes more anchored as a visitor scrolls, and structural rules subtly connect around active content. Interactions never rely on surprise, spectacle, or inaccessible hover-only behaviour.

## Animation

The initial hero image will reveal behind a clipping frame while structural rules settle horizontally and headline lines enter with a short offset fade. Programme items may extend a line and reveal their description when focused or opened. Image crops can drift no more than a few pixels during scroll. Motion must remain under 300 ms for UI states, use transforms and opacity only, and fully respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Typography System

**Space Grotesk** provides the strong, engineered display voice for the event identity and major headings. **DM Sans** provides clear, neutral body copy and form language. Technical metadata uses DM Mono (or the tabular-number feature where appropriate) at compact sizes with deliberate tracking. Headings use tight leading and varied scale; body text stays generous, readable, and never becomes a wall of uppercase.

## Brand Essence

**AAK Annual Convention 2026 is Kenya’s essential built-environment gathering for practitioners who want to move ideas, policy, and practice from fragility toward resilience.**

**Personality:** rigorous, grounded, forward-looking.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are precise, declarative, and intellectually confident. Calls to action are directional rather than generic, and microcopy is calm and useful.

> "Trace the conversations shaping resilient practice."

> "Choose your place in the Convention."

## Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark uses a condensed, stacked `AAK / CONVENTION / 2026` lock-up integrated with a distinctive **interlocking structural-span symbol**. The mark is a bold graphic with three offset vertical components meeting at a stable horizontal bridge, communicating fragmentation becoming connection. It is used as a non-text icon in the header and favicon; the wordmark itself remains HTML typography for clarity and accessibility.

## Signature Brand Color

**AAK Oxide — #8B2635.** A deep, mineral burgundy inspired by the current AAK visual family and chosen for its association with material, civic conviction, and structural permanence.

## Style Decisions

- **Color discipline:** AAK Oxide (`#8B2635`) is the single warm accent for calls to action, active states, numerals, and moments of tension. Deep coastal teal is reserved for rare contextual emphasis; pink or rose variations are not used.
- **Tectonic rules:** Fragmented lines must resolve into a meaningful anchor—an image edge, metadata band, section index, programme row, or caption—so that they communicate structure in transition rather than act as generic background decoration.
- **Page identity:** The programme reads as a working schedule with a live-day index; the theme reads as a civic position paper with an explicit fragility-to-resilience sequence; and the venue page connects its linework to place, climate, and material context.
- **Attendee-facing guidance:** All public copy speaks to Convention visitors in calm institutional language. Registration guidance explains the trusted AAK journey and its visitor outcomes without exposing implementation terminology.

# Technical Tours: Verified Research Notes

## Mwache Multipurpose Dam Development Project

The Coast Development Authority identifies the project in **Kwale County** and describes a project scope spanning dam construction, catchment management, and capacity building. Its stated goal is to reduce siltation and extend the economic life of the proposed Mwache reservoir through catchment restoration interventions. [Coast Development Authority](https://www.cda.go.ke/mwache_dam)

## Ukunda Airstrip, Diani

Cementers Ltd documents work for the Kenya Airports Authority involving a **310-metre runway extension**, **10,200 square metres of apron extension**, a new fire station, and Air Traffic Control Tower relocation. [Cementers Ltd](https://cementers.com/project/extension-of-runway-apron-ukunda-airstrip-diani/)

## Kisite Mpunguti Marine Park and Wasini Island

The Convention programme names Kisite Mpunguti Marine Park and Wasini Island as an official technical-tour location.
